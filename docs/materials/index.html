Materials

The project's published documents, "Model Instruments for Data Center Development in Florida Counties". The current release is hosted here. Every release is also kept at Zenodo, a public archive where anyone may deposit work; a record there is not a review. Each record has a DOI, a permanent address to cite.

Known gaps: no county has adopted the model documents, no lawyer has reviewed them, and there is no organization yet.

Current release: 2026.4

Published on 6 November 2026, in two records. Record 1 holds the model instruments: the ordinances, agreements and forms themselves (CC0 1.0, free to use without credit). Record 2 holds the guide, the open legal questions and the methodology (CC BY 4.0, free to use with credit and a note of any changes).
