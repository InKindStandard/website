COMMUNITY BENEFITS AGREEMENT

This Community Benefits Agreement (this "Agreement") is made on [date] between [DEVELOPER ENTITY], a [entity type] authorized to do business in Florida (the "Developer"), and [COMMUNITY PARTY], a Florida not-for-profit corporation (the "Community Party"). [FEE OWNER], the owner of the Project Site if different from the Developer, joins this Agreement to subject its interest in the Project Site to Article XII.

RECITALS

A. The Developer proposes to develop the Project described in Exhibit A on the Project Site in [COUNTY] County, Florida, and has applied or will apply for the Approvals.

B. The Approvals will be decided by the County and other public bodies under their own regulations, at public hearings.

C. The Community Party is a Florida nonprofit corporation that serves residents of [COUNTY] County.

D. The Parties enter into this Agreement so that residents share in the Project's productive capacity and employment, and so that what the Developer will provide is published and enforceable.

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

ARTICLE II. THE COUNTY AND THE APPROVALS

Section 2.1 The County is not a party.

(a) [COUNTY] County is not a party to this Agreement and is not a third-party beneficiary of it. The County has no right, duty or role under this Agreement, including any right or duty to enforce it, and this Agreement creates no obligation to or right in the County.

(b) No approval, election, declaration, determination, notice, consent, designation, appointment, audit, filing, custody, ownership, or receipt of property or money under this Agreement is given to, made by or held by the County. The County does not select, approve, pay, instruct or receive the report of any valuer.

(c) The Appointing Authority, the Custodian and each valuer and neutral under this Agreement are not the County or any other local government. No Fund Holder, institution named in Exhibit C or Recipient is a Public Approver.

Section 2.2 No use in the approval process.

(a) Neither Party has asked or will ask the County to require, negotiate, review, approve or consider this Agreement, and neither Party files it in the County's application file. Neither Party will represent to the County that any approval should be granted, or granted sooner, because of this Agreement.

(b) This Agreement is not a condition of any development order, permit, rezoning, plan amendment, development agreement or other approval.

(c) No grant under this Agreement is conditioned on, or timed to, any County decision. Before the Approvals Date, the Community Party announces or promises no grant.

Section 2.3 Regulation unaffected. Nothing in this Agreement waives, relaxes or substitutes for any standard of the County's land development regulations, any development agreement between the Developer and the County, or any state or federal permit. Where this Agreement and any of them address the same subject, each applies by its own terms. A reference in this Agreement to a County standard means a standard that applies to the Project by its own force, and it gives the County no role under this Agreement. This Agreement adds to, and does not displace, any procurement, public records or contracting law that binds a purchaser or Recipient that is a public body.

Section 2.4 No public money; no political use.

(a) No payment, capacity, asset or other benefit under this Agreement is made, delivered or granted to a Public Approver or to a facility a Public Approver owns or operates. No Permitted Purpose, and no commitment under Article VIII or Section 7.7, is administered by a Public Approver or by a department or instrumentality of one, or runs to a Public Approver.

(b) No payment under this Agreement goes to any public official or political committee, and none funds lobbying or political activity.

(c) No Community Payment, Readiness Payment, Administration Payment or Grant Administration Payment, no payment under Section 13.4, and no disbursement from the Community Benefit Account, the Community Fund or the Readiness Account is made to a Barred Person.

Section 2.5 No public subsidy or tax benefit.

(a) The Developer shall not seek, claim or accept, and shall cause its affiliates not to seek, claim or accept, any credit, offset, abatement, exemption, refund or other reduction of any tax, fee, assessment, payment in lieu of taxes, rent or other charge owed to the County, any other local government or the State on account of any payment, capacity or other benefit under this Agreement. The Developer shall not cite, or permit an affiliate to cite, any valuation under this Agreement or Module Schedule 3 in support of such a request.

(b) Subsection (a) does not limit the deduction, depreciation or other ordinary treatment, for federal or state income tax purposes, of any payment under this Agreement or of the cost of providing any capacity or other benefit under it, except as subsection (d) provides, and does not bar any exemption, credit or other treatment that would be available to the Developer on the same terms if this Agreement did not exist. This subsection does not reach an exemption, classification or assessment, including under s. 196.26 or s. 193.501, Florida Statutes, of land subject to an easement or other interest granted under this Agreement, which subsection (a) bars.

(c) The Developer shall not seek or accept, and shall cause its affiliates not to seek or accept, any rebate, reimbursement, grant or other payment measured by, or paid from, tax increment attributable to the Project Site, including a payment from a redevelopment trust fund under s. 163.387, Florida Statutes, whether or not it would be available if this Agreement did not exist.

(d) The Developer claims no charitable contribution deduction, and causes its affiliates to claim none, for any payment, capacity, easement or other property provided under this Agreement, including an Instruction Share, and requests no acknowledgment that it received no goods or services for any of them.

(e) The Community Payment for the year in which the Developer or an affiliate receives a reduction or payment this Section bars increases by its amount, less any part repaid to the public body that allowed or made it; under a capacity Election, the Developer instead pays that amount into the Restricted Account within [__] days after receiving the reduction or payment. Breach of this Section is a default.

ARTICLE III. THE COMMUNITY PARTY AND ITS SUPPORT

Section 3.1 Community Party representations. The Community Party represents that it (a) is a Florida nonprofit corporation in good standing; (b) has operated in [COUNTY] County for at least [__] years; (c) has authorized this Agreement by board resolution, attached; (d) has a written conflict-of-interest policy; (e) has published a statement of its membership and how its board is chosen; and (f) has published, for each of its [__] most recent fiscal years, the name of each funder that provided [__] percent or more of its revenue in that year. It attaches the same statement for each later fiscal year during the term to the next annual report under Section 4.3.

Section 3.2 Payments to the Community Party. The Community Party receives no payment from the Developer, and no disbursement from the Custodian, under this Agreement except: (a) the Grant Administration Payment; (b) a grant Section 5.9(c) permits; (c) after the Community Payments end, the costs Section 13.4(b) permits; (d) where it is a Qualified Operator that has become the Recipient, the payments this Agreement and the Module make to the Recipient, except that from the Readiness Account it receives only its own costs under Section 6.2(b)(ii); and (e) Community Payments held as its restricted fund in the Community Benefit Account under Section 5.8(a), applied only under Sections 5.9 to 5.11. No payment or disbursement under this Agreement pays its general operating costs.

Section 3.3 Support letters. For each public hearing on an Approval, the Community Party shall deliver a letter stating its organizational position in support of the Project as described in Exhibit A, not later than [__] days after the Developer's written request and in any event before the hearing, if the request is made at least [__] days before the hearing. The Community Party's support is for that description only. Before delivering a letter, the Community Party registers or reports under any local lobbyist rule that applies, and discloses this Agreement as that rule requires.

Section 3.4 Content of letters.

(a) Each letter states the Community Party's support and its own reasons. It does not certify that the Project meets any standard, and it does not describe the benefits of this Agreement beyond the disclosures this Section requires.

(b) Each letter discloses that the Community Party is a party to an agreement with the Developer under which it holds rights to community benefits and the Developer funds independent monitoring. It also discloses, as they apply:

(i) where the Election includes a Community Payment, that the Community Party directs an annual payment from the Developer, held by an independent custodian, to community grants under a published policy; or, under the Community Fund form, that the Developer makes an annual payment to a community foundation fund whose grants the foundation decides; or, where Exhibit A states no form but names a Fund Holder, that the payment will be held as a fund of that foundation, whose grants the foundation decides, if it accepts in time, and otherwise by an independent custodian for grants the Community Party directs under a published policy;

(ii) where the Community Party receives a Grant Administration Payment or is the Recipient, that the Developer pays its documented cost of administering those grants or the Allocation; and

(iii) where the Parties choose Module Section 3.6 and the Election includes a share in kind, that the Host Institution, if it is an accredited public institution and accepts, holds an interest under this Agreement in an annual payment from the Developer for instruction that uses the Allocation, which that institution alone administers and in which the Community Party has no interest or role; and, where the Parties choose that Section and the Election includes a share paid as the Community Payment, that the accredited public institution named in Exhibit C under Module Section 3.6(h), if it accepts, holds the same kind of interest in an annual payment for instruction that uses computing bought with grants of that payment.

(c) Exhibit E is the form of letter.

Section 3.5 Suspension. The duty to deliver a letter is suspended (a) for any hearing at which the County's staff report finds that the Project does not meet an applicable standard, until that finding is resolved; and (b) while any default of the Developer under this Agreement, including under Section 2.2 or 4.2, continues after notice and the cure period under Section 11.1, until the default is cured.

Section 3.6 Delivery to the record. Letters are delivered to the County's official record for the hearing, with a copy to the Developer, and not to individual commissioners, as s. 286.0115, Florida Statutes, and the County's procedures provide.

Section 3.7 The organization speaks only for itself. This Article binds only the Community Party's official organizational position. Its members, its directors and staff acting in their own names, and every other person remain free to speak, comment, testify, petition, appeal and sue as they choose, and the Community Party has no duty to restrain or discipline any of them. The Community Party gives no covenant not to oppose, no release of any claim, and no waiver of any right to comment, appeal, seek enforcement of any standard, or sue, as to this Project or any other.

Section 3.8 Material Change.

(a) If, after signing, the Developer applies for or obtains any of the following (each a "Material Change"), the Community Party may withdraw its support by notice: (i) an increase in Approved Capacity beyond Exhibit A; (ii) any use of groundwater for heat rejection, or any increase in permitted water withdrawal; (iii) a variance, waiver, deviation or alternative compliance from any standard of the County's land development regulations or of any development agreement that applies to the Project; (iv) addition of land to the Project Site; (v) on-site generation not described in Exhibit A; or (vi) [other].

(b) While an application for a Material Change is pending, the duty under Section 3.3 is suspended.

(c) Before applying for a Material Change, the Developer may give the Community Party notice of it, and the Parties shall then confer for not more than [__] days on whether support continues.

Section 3.9 Missing letters; benefits continue.

(a) If the Community Party fails to deliver a letter under Section 3.3, the Developer's sole remedy is to terminate this Agreement by notice before Articles V to VIII become operative under Section 13.1(c).

(b) After Articles V to VIII become operative, no failure under this Article excuses any obligation of the Developer. Withdrawal of support under Section 3.8 ends only the Community Party's duties under Sections 3.3 to 3.8. If the Developer proceeds with a Project that includes a Material Change, Articles V to VIII continue to apply and scale to the Project as built.

Section 3.10 Tax and lobbying compliance. Each Party is responsible for its own compliance with federal tax law on lobbying, political activity and private benefit, and with any local lobbyist registration rule. Nothing in this Agreement requires the Community Party to take any action that would jeopardize its tax-exempt status. No Party treats any payment under this Agreement as a charitable contribution, and no receipt the Community Party, a Fund Holder or a Recipient gives for one states that no goods or services were provided.

Section 3.11 Successor Community Party.

(a) Succession Event. A "Succession Event" is the Community Party's dissolution other than by administrative dissolution, its administrative dissolution not cured by reinstatement within [__] days, its ceasing to operate in [COUNTY] County, its merger into another entity (in which case the survivor holds only the rights subsection (c) describes, for enforcement only, in place of an institution under that subsection, unless it meets and assumes under subsection (b)) or, as to its rights and duties under Sections 5.2 to 5.14 only, its failure to adopt or follow the Grant Policy that it does not cure under Section 11.1.

(b) Named successor. On a Succession Event, the Community Party's rights and duties under this Agreement and the account agreement under Section 10.2, other than under Sections 3.3 to 3.8, pass to the successor its board has named in advance by notice to the Developer, the Custodian and each Recipient, if that successor meets Sections 3.1(a), (b), (d), (e) and (f) and 4.2(b) and assumes them in writing, authorized by resolution of its board, within [__] days after the event. In the same writing the successor may also assume the Community Party's duties under Sections 3.3 to 3.8. The successor is then the Community Party. Each notice naming a successor is a present assignment, effective on a Succession Event, of the rights this Section passes, and the Developer consents to it.

(c) No named successor. Failing such a successor, the first institution named in Exhibit C, and then the second, that is not then the Recipient and accepts in writing by notice to the Developer and the Custodian (i) holds, for enforcement only, the Community Party's rights under Articles IX to XI and the account agreement under Section 10.2, including its rights to give notice of default, to certify a draw under Article IX, to replace the Custodian under Section 10.2(d) and to engage the reviewers, accountants and counsel whose costs Section 10.1 pays; and (ii) holds the Community Party's rights and duties under Sections 5.2 to 5.14, for the same purposes.

(d) Effect. The Community Party's duties under Sections 3.3 to 3.8 end on the passage, unless a successor assumes them under subsection (b). If they end before every hearing on the Approvals has been held, Section 3.9(a) applies as if a letter had not been delivered. The Developer publishes notice of the passage under Section 4.1.

ARTICLE IV. PUBLICATION AND REPORTING

(a) Not later than [__] days before the first public hearing on the Project's development agreement or any Approval, each Party publishes this Agreement, with every exhibit and the completed form of the Module, on a public website.

(b) Each Party keeps this Agreement, every amendment, each annual report under Section 4.3, each audit made under this Agreement and each notice of default, with its cure, posted on a public website until the wind-down under Section 13.4 is complete. The Developer also delivers a copy of each, for public access, to the first institution named in Exhibit C.

(c) Where this Agreement requires a statement or finding to be published before an act, the person making it posts it on a public website, with notice to the Parties and the Recipient, not less than [__] days before the act, and attaches it to its next report or statement under Section 4.3. Where this Agreement requires one to be published after an act, the person this Agreement names to publish it, or otherwise the person making it, posts it the same way within [__] days after the act.

Section 4.2 No side agreements.

(a) No term of the Parties' relationship concerning the Project is omitted from the published Agreement. The Parties represent that no other agreement, payment or understanding between the Developer or an affiliate and the Community Party, or any of its directors, officers or staff, relates to the Project, except as Exhibit A discloses.

(b) The Community Party receives no funding, gift, loan, sponsorship or in-kind support from the Developer or an affiliate other than under this Agreement, and will accept none during the term unless it has first been published under Section 4.1. Acceptance of support that has not first been so published is a default of the Community Party and, where the Developer or an affiliate gave the support, of the Developer, and it excuses no obligation of the Developer.

(c) Any later agreement, payment, gift, loan, sponsorship, in-kind support or understanding, other than under this Agreement and other than support that subsection (b) governs, between the Developer or an affiliate and the Community Party, whether or not it relates to the Project, and any later one relating to the Project between the Developer or an affiliate and any director, officer or staff member of the Community Party, that is not published under Section 4.1 within [__] days after it is made is a default.

Section 4.3 Annual report.

(a) Each year by [date], the Developer shall publish a plain-language report stating, for the prior year: each commitment under Articles V to VIII and its status, including each workforce and training commitment and its delivery as the receiving institution reports it under Section 7.5; the construction labor hours Section 7.5 requires; the equipment statement Module paragraph T-16 describes, which the Developer gives under every Election; any default and cure; any change to the incentives Exhibit A, item 9, lists; the receipts and uses of each Account, as the Custodian reports them; each Administration Payment, Grant Administration Payment and Instruction Share payment; and the count of reports Section 4.5(h) requires. Where any share is delivered in kind, the report includes the report Module Section 1.22(a) requires. A commitment under Articles VI to VIII to deliver, install or convey equipment, an improvement or an interest in land is reported as delivered only where the institution or facility owner that receives it has confirmed in writing that it is delivered and accepted, and the confirmation is attached; until then it is reported as in progress.

(b) The Recipient attaches its budget and statement of costs under Section 5.17(c), and may attach its own statement. The Instruction Institution attaches its budget, its statement of costs and its report under Module Section 3.6(e) and Section 7.5.

(c) Where a Community Payment is made, the Community Party attaches a statement of the Community Benefit Account's receipts and every disbursement, naming each grantee, its amount and its Permitted Purpose, the Reserve Share, any share granted for durable assets under Section 5.14 and any finding and carry-forward under it, with each comparison, statement and finding Section 5.16 requires; or, under the Community Fund form, the Fund Holder's statement of the same for the Community Fund.

(d) The report opens with a table that lists each commitment under Articles V to VIII, including each in Exhibit A, item 6, by its section or item, without restating it. For each, the table states its due date or trigger; its status as not yet due, in progress, delivered (with any confirmation subsection (a) requires), in default, cured, or not applicable; and where in the report or its attachments the evidence is.

Section 4.4 Copies of filings. The Developer delivers to the Community Party a copy of each application, study, report and response that the Developer, or any person acting for it, files with a Public Approver on an Approval or a Replacement Approval: at signing, for each filed before then, and afterward when it is filed. The Developer may withhold only a portion exempt from public disclosure under chapter 119, Florida Statutes, and states for each portion withheld the exemption it claims.

Section 4.5 Annual meeting; contacts; reports.

(a) Annual meeting. Each year, within [sixty (60)] days after the report under Section 4.3 is published or, if it is not published by the date Section 4.3(a) sets, after that date, beginning with the first report due after the Approvals Date, the Developer holds a meeting open to the public, on a date set after consulting the Community Party, starting between [6:00 p.m.] and [7:00 p.m.] on a weekday that is not a legal holiday, at a place in [COUNTY] County [within [__] miles of the Project Site] that is accessible to persons with disabilities and that no Public Approver owns or operates (Section 2.4(a))[, with a way to take part remotely]. The Developer presents the report and answers questions about it. The Community Party may present its own statement, and each Recipient and the Instruction Institution, where there is one, may present its own report. Not less than [fourteen (14)] days before the meeting, the Developer posts notice of it, stating its date, time and place[, how to take part remotely] and an address for written questions, under subsection (g), and delivers the notice to the Community Party, each Recipient and the Instruction Institution, where there is one. The Developer pays the reasonable cost of the place[, the remote participation], the notice and the summary under subsection (c), but pays nothing for the place to the Community Party (Section 3.2) or to a Barred Person.

(b) Questions. Any person may submit a written question about the report or this Agreement for the meeting, at the address its notice states, from the posting of the notice until the meeting, or at the meeting. ARTICLE XIV. GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 14.1 Relationship. The Parties are independent. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency.

Section 14.2 Law and venue. Florida law governs this Agreement. Venue lies in the circuit court for [COUNTY] County.

Section 14.3 Amendment. This Agreement is amended only in writing signed by the Developer, the Community Party and every Recipient then holding rights and, for an amendment that affects an Instruction Share due or to become due to an Instruction Institution or a former Instruction Institution, that institution. An amendment takes effect only when published under Section 4.1.

Section 14.4 Severability. If any provision is held invalid, the rest continues. If a court holds that any provision makes the County a party or makes this Agreement a condition of any approval, that provision is severed and the rest continues.

Section 14.5 Conflicts of interest. Each director and officer of the Community Party who has any financial relationship with the Developer or an affiliate discloses it in writing to the Community Party's board and takes no part in any decision of the Community Party under this Agreement, and the disclosure is published under Section 4.1. A director or officer with a financial interest in an applicant for a grant, or with a relationship this Section describes, discloses it and takes no part in the decision on that grant.

Section 14.6 Nothing reverts. Nothing paid into or held in an Account reverts to the Developer, except the unapplied balance of cash security under Section 9.3 and the balances Module Section 1.26(e) states.

Section 14.7 No waiver. No failure or delay in enforcing any provision of this Agreement waives it, and no waiver is effective unless it is in writing, signed by the person giving it and published under Section 4.1.

Section 14.8 Notices; counterparts; authority. A notice under this Agreement is in writing and is given by hand delivery, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by electronic mail followed by a copy by courier, to the addresses in Exhibit A, or in a person's acceptance, as updated by notice. A notice is given when delivered or, if delivery is refused, when refused; a notice by electronic mail is given when sent, if the courier copy is sent not later than the next business day. A copy of every notice of default goes to the Custodian, each Recipient, any Fund Holder and each lender entitled to notice under Section 12.4. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature. Each signatory represents that it is authorized to sign, and board resolutions are attached.

Section 14.9 Entire agreement. This Agreement, with its Exhibits, the Module provisions that form part of it, the agreements made under it and any agreement disclosed in Exhibit A or published as Section 4.2 requires, is the entire agreement of the Parties concerning the Project and supersedes every prior understanding between them.
