EXHIBIT “I”: FORM OF EMERGENCY EQUIPMENT LEASE

(Module M2, Section M2.1(c)(2). Attached only where the Board elects Module M2. Use one lease for each Designated Emergency Facility.)

THIS EMERGENCY EQUIPMENT LEASE is made as of [DATE] between [DEVELOPER ENTITY NAME] (the “Developer”), as lessor, and [COUNTY NAME] County, Florida, a political subdivision of the State of Florida, by and through its Board of County Commissioners (the “County”), as lessee, under Section M2.1(c)(2) of [the Host Community Development Agreement and Declaration of Restrictive Covenants Running with the Land] [the ground lease] between them dated [DATE] (the “Instrument”) and s. 125.031, Florida Statutes. Terms defined in the Instrument or in Module M2 have the same meanings here.

  1. 1. Equipment and premises. The Developer leases to the County the emergency power equipment described in Schedule 1 (the “Equipment”), installed at [NAME AND ADDRESS OF DESIGNATED EMERGENCY FACILITY] (the “Emergency Facility”), premises the County [owns] [leases, other than as a public shelter] [operates or designates as a public shelter under the written agreement with its owner dated [DATE], which states who holds title to the Equipment and, from 1 January 2027, comports with s. 163.3177(6)(h), Florida Statutes]. The Equipment is connected behind the meter serving the Emergency Facility and is operated only to serve the Emergency Facility’s own load.
  1. 2. Term. This lease runs for [__] years from [DATE], a term within the period s. 125.031, Florida Statutes, allows. Not less than [__] days before it ends, the Developer shall deliver to the County a successive lease in this form, for approval by the Board, so that the leases continue for the life of the use. If the Board has not approved a successor lease when this lease ends, this lease continues from month to month on its terms, but not beyond the date on which it would exceed the period s. 125.031, Florida Statutes, allows, as Section M2.1(d) of the Instrument provides.
  1. 3. Rent. The County pays a fixed nominal rent of US$[__] a year, payable only from non-ad valorem revenues. The County makes no other payment under this lease. The rent does not vary with the electricity the Equipment produces, the hours it runs, or the Developer’s costs.
  1. 4. Maintenance, testing, and fuel. The Developer maintains, repairs, and replaces the Equipment at its own cost. The Developer arranges and pays directly for testing, by load bank or by an independent contractor it engages, including the readiness test under Section M2.1(b) of the Instrument. For fuel-dependent Equipment, the Developer delivers fuel to the Emergency Facility’s tank at its own cost, title passing on delivery, and the on-site tank capacity is sufficient for the full period stated in the Impact Findings, so that no delivery is needed during an event. The Developer performs the air permit and emission rule duties that Section M2.1(j) of the Instrument assigns to it.
  1. 5. Use. The County Emergency Management Director decides when and how the Equipment is used, within the limits of Section M2.1(f) and (j) of the Instrument.
  1. 6. No sale or delivery of electricity. This lease involves no sale of electricity. The Developer delivers no electricity from the Property to any other premises, and the Equipment serves only the load of the Emergency Facility, behind the meter serving it.
  1. 7. No County cost. The County bears no capital, operating, maintenance, fuel, or replacement cost, gives no indemnity, and pledges no credit. The Developer indemnifies the County under the Instrument for the installation, operation, and maintenance of the Equipment. Nothing in this lease waives the County’s sovereign immunity or the limits of s. 768.28, Florida Statutes.
  1. 8. Ownership. The Developer owns the Equipment. The County holds only its leasehold, and does not by this lease become a joint owner with, or stockholder of, the Developer, or pledge its credit, in violation of Article VII, Section 10 of the Florida Constitution.
  1. 9. Access. The County gives the Developer and its contractors access to the Emergency Facility, on reasonable notice and subject to the County’s security procedures, for installation, maintenance, testing, and fueling.
  1. 10. Next form. If the Equipment is conveyed to the County under Section M2.1(c)(3) of the Instrument, this lease ends on the conveyance, and Section M2.1 of the Instrument then governs.
  1. 11. Instrument controls; approval. If this lease conflicts with the Instrument, the Instrument controls. This lease is governed by Florida law and takes effect on its approval by the Board, as s. 125.031, Florida Statutes, requires.
