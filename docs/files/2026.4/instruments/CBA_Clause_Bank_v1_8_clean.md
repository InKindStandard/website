# Community Benefits Agreement Clause Bank

## ARTICLE VII. WORKFORCE AND LOCAL OPPORTUNITY

**Section 7.7 Optional terms.**

(a) **Automation transition.** This subsection applies to the Developer's own employees at the Project Site and, through item (x), to the employees of its operating contractors.

(i) **Definitions.** An "automation-related elimination" is the elimination of a full-time position at the Project Site, without a replacement position in the same occupation there within [ninety (90)] days, where the introduction or expansion of machinery, robotics, software or artificial intelligence systems at the Project Site or for its operations is a cause. A position eliminated within [twelve (12)] months after the Developer installs or expands such systems to perform tasks of that position is presumed to be an automation-related elimination unless the Developer shows otherwise in its report under Agreement Section 4.3. An "affected employee" is an employee whose position is subject to an automation-related elimination.

(ii) **Notice.** At least [one hundred eighty (180)] days before automation-related eliminations of [__] or more positions at the Project Site within any twelve-month period, the Developer gives written notice to the Community Party, to each affected employee and, where the regional workforce board is not a department or instrumentality of the County, to the board, stating the number of positions, their occupations and the expected dates, and whether automation, including artificial intelligence or other software, is a cause and, if it is, the technology. This notice is in addition to any notice the Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act requires (29 U.S.C. 2101 et seq.). Where that Act applies, the Developer also gives a notice that meets it, and its period runs within the period of this notice (29 U.S.C. 2105).

(iii) **First right to new roles.** For [twelve (12)] months after an affected employee's position ends, before hiring from outside for an open position at the Project Site, the Developer offers it to the affected employee if the employee is qualified for it or can become qualified through training of [__] weeks or less.

(iv) **Paid retraining.** The Developer offers each affected employee, at the employee's choice, either [__] hours of training for a position at the Project Site, or fewer if the employee chooses, paid at the employee's regular wage, or the tuition and fees, up to $[__], of programs the employee chooses at [STATE COLLEGE] or another eligible training provider, usable within [eighteen (18)] months after the position ends. Training paid by public workforce funds does not count toward this item.

(v) **[Severance.** The Developer pays each affected employee who is not placed in another position at the Project Site severance of at least [__] weeks' pay for each year of service, and not less than [__] weeks' pay in total, by that formula alone. Each payment is accompanied by a plain statement that, under s. 443.101(3)(b), Florida Statutes, severance and reemployment assistance run one after the other.]

(vi) **Monitoring notice.** On hiring, and before any new kind of electronic monitoring begins, the Developer gives each employee at the Project Site written notice of the kinds of electronic monitoring it uses, and posts the notice where employees can see it.

(vii) **Human review.** No employee at the Project Site is discharged on the basis of an automated system's output without review by a person with authority to reverse the decision, and the employee may give information for that review.

(viii) **Collective bargaining.** Where employees at the Project Site are covered by a collective bargaining agreement, a written certification signed by their employer and the employees' representative that the agreement provides terms equal to or better than this subsection meets it for those employees. Nothing in this subsection displaces any employer's duty to bargain over how it carries out these terms.

(ix) **Reporting.** The Developer states in its report under Agreement Section 4.3 the number of automation-related eliminations in the year, by occupation, and the offers made under items (iii) and (iv), without the name, wage or other identifying data of any person.

(x) **Operating contractors.** The Developer requires each contractor whose employees work at the Project Site on a regular schedule in its operation, maintenance or security to give those employees the terms of items (i) to (viii) as if it were the Developer, and to give the Developer what item (ix) requires.

(b) **Wage floor.** The Developer pays each of its own employees on the Project Site not less than [__].

(c) **Community child care.** [Terms to be drafted] [Scope: licensed child care slots for residents, paid directly to the local early learning coalition or to licensed providers, on the terms of Agreement Section 5.10(a)(ii).]

(d) [Reserved. Agreement Section 11.3(c) governs enforcement of subsections (a) and (b).]

## ARTICLE VIII. OTHER COMMITMENTS

**Section 8.2 Recovered heat.** The Developer grants the Recipient, for the life of the use, a right of first offer to take thermal energy the Project would otherwise reject, at no charge for the energy itself, through any thermal interface the Project provides, including any that a County standard or development agreement requires, with all facilities beyond the interface at the offtaker's cost and no warranty of quantity or temperature. The Developer shall offer recovered heat that the Recipient does not take, at least once every [__] years, to each agricultural, aquacultural or commercial offtaker named in Exhibit A and, by a published solicitation, to any other, and shall report each year, in its report under Section 4.3, each offer made, each response received and the recoverable thermal output.

**Section 8.3 Retired equipment.** The Developer grants the Recipient a right of first refusal on computing equipment retired from the Project, after verified data sanitization, on the terms of Module paragraph T-15. The repair terms of Section 7.4 apply.

**Section 8.4 Fiber to anchor institutions.** Where the Developer trenches within [__] feet of a school, college, library or nonprofit health clinic named in Exhibit A that is not owned or operated by a Public Approver, it shall offer that institution, directly, [__] dark-fiber strands or a lateral on the terms Exhibit A states. No offer under this Section is a condition of, or compensation for, the use of any public road or right-of-way.

**Section 8.5 Backup power and communications at community facilities.**

(a) The Developer provides backup power equipment for use at each facility Exhibit A names: [schools of [SCHOOL DISTRICT], including schools used as public shelters or as cooling or warming centers] [and] [cooling and warming centers and other community facilities that no Public Approver owns or operates], on the terms Exhibit A states. Exhibit A names no facility that is a "Designated Emergency Facility" under a development agreement or ground lease to which the County is a party, and no facility that an impact fee study of the County identifies for emergency power. The equipment is installed with transfer equipment that prevents backfeed to the utility system and is sized to carry the critical loads Exhibit A states for each facility, including cooling or heating where the facility serves as a cooling or warming center, for the run time Exhibit A states, with fuel or stored energy on site for that run time.

(b) [Where Exhibit A so states, the Developer also provides at each named facility satellite backup communications: terminals, power for them that does not depend on the grid, and paid service for [__] years, replacing any terminal that fails or is destroyed during that period.]

(c) The Developer maintains, tests, and refuels the equipment, and keeps any service in force, on the terms Exhibit A states. The owner of each facility takes the equipment and any service by its own written acceptance and confirms each delivery in writing as Section 4.3(a) of the Agreement provides.

(d) Nothing in this Section requires a facility to be designated or used as a shelter or as a cooling or warming center, and no Public Approver has any right under this Section or administers the equipment.

**Section 8.6 First offer on the site.** If an Abandonment occurs, the Developer shall offer the Recipient [and a member-governed cooperative named in Exhibit A whose governing instruments bar any distribution of earnings or assets to a member] the first opportunity to acquire the Project Site and shell improvements at appraised value before marketing them to others. The offer is subject to any public purchase option recorded against the Project Site, which takes priority. This Section does not apply to a purchase by a public agency.
