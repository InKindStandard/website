# PART G. PLAN POLICY ON DATA CENTER WATER SOURCES

Since 1 July 2026 a water management district "may not issue a permit to a large-scale data center applicant for an allocation of water if the proposed use of the water is harmful to the water resources of the area or is prohibited by the applicable local government zoning regulations and comprehensive plan" (s. 373.262(2), Florida Statutes, created by Chapter 2026-65, Laws of Florida). It does not reach potable water a public water system supplies, or groundwater a third party withdraws under its own permit.
