Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to read this site, including people with disabilities.

The standard we apply

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, and a text contrast of at least 7 to 1. We aim to meet it, and cannot yet say the site does.

What has been tested

  • Automated checks, 18 November 2026, found no problem. One was axe-core 4.14, an open accessibility checker, run on every page in light and dark colors. The others were the site's own checks, for color contrast, page structure, links within the site, line length, and anything cut off at phone and desktop widths.
  • By hand, 13 November 2026, on the earlier design of each page, in Edge on Windows: no problem was found with keyboard, the Narrator screen reader, zoom to 400 percent, larger text and spacing, and print. Not yet tested by hand: this design, and a phone.

AI tools help build and check this site, and the founder reviews every page and decides what is published, except work the founder steps aside from because of a tie. No outside reviewer has audited the site.

Color, movement and scripts

A light and a dark theme are offered; the buttons in the header choose one, or follow your device. Your browser keeps the choice on your device.

Cards lift, sections fade in once as you scroll, numbers count up once and a drawing draws itself once. Each takes under two seconds, nothing flashes, and none of it moves if your device asks for reduced motion. Every page reads, links and prints in full without scripts.

Known problems

The hosted documents are the release's own plain text and Markdown files. A browser shows them as unformatted text, and may show them small on a phone or download them. That is harder to read, most of all with a screen reader. An edition made for reading in a browser is planned.

Any problem we find will be listed here. The project's page "{{screen:title}}" is separate and not covered here.

Report a barrier, or ask for another format

Write to contact@inkindstandard.org. Say which page and what happened; we will work with you to get the content in a form you can use.

This statement applies from the date below.
