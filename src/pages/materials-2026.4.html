Documents in Record 1

Each instrument comes as two files. The annotated copy opens with a heading block on what the instrument is and does, what to settle before use and its known gaps, and keeps the drafting notes. The clean copy is the same text with the heading block and the drafting notes removed, the starting point for a county's own draft. The Drafter's Notes and the About file come once.
