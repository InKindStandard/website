Contact

Write to contact@inkindstandard.org. You need not give your name; send only what your question needs.

What we answer

  • Questions about what the model documents say and how they work.
  • Corrections to anything the project has published.
  • Problems using this site, and requests for another format.
  • Requests to see, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you.

Complaints about our own work

Write to the same address if you think our work takes a side, or to complain about our work or the people who do it. Today the founder reads every message, complaints about the founder included. From the project's list of known gaps:

"Because the founder prepared and approved all the published work, every complaint about it waits, with no date set, for an advisory committee not yet formed; the founder is looking for its members. Reports of factual errors that show the official record or text are checked and, if wrong, corrected now."

What we do not do

  • We give no advice on anyone's own legal situation. For that, ask a lawyer.
  • We do not urge any county to decide a particular way.
  • We enforce nothing, and take up no complaints about a company or a county. Those go to the public body concerned.

The privacy statement says what happens to your message.
