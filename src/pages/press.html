The project in brief, and how to cite it

In brief

What In-Kind Standard is

A nonpartisan, educational project that gives communities and their local governments free model documents, plain explanations and public records about large projects, starting with data centers, so that they can decide knowing the facts. It is citizen-prepared and planned to become a charitable nonprofit. It is not a government body, a law firm, an advocacy group or a developer, and it enforces nothing.

Citation

How to cite the model documents

Cite each release by its permanent address (DOI) at Zenodo, and name the version you used. The current release, its DOIs and how to cite each document are on the release page.
