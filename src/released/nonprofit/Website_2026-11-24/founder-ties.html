What this is. The list of the ties of the founder of In-Kind Standard, and of the founder's own decisions on them while the advisory committee cannot act, kept under the Conflict of Interest Policy, sections 3.4 and 9. Section numbers given without a policy's name are that policy's. It will be shown on the project's main website, on the page that says who prepares the work, once that website is live. Until then, and afterwards too, it is sent to anyone who asks (contact@inkindstandard.org). Each version passes the Neutrality Test (Political Neutrality Policy, section 3.13) before it is shown or sent. It names no household member and no private individual other than the founder. "The Record" means the County Record, the project's public record of what Florida counties and cities have done about the kinds of large facilities it has entries on, such as data centers.

Known gaps. No organization has adopted the Conflict of Interest Policy, and no lawyer has reviewed it. The advisory committee has not been formed, so no one independent of the founder has checked the list of ties or reviewed any decision below; each decision waits for its review (section 9, point 3). The founder's first yearly statement (section 6) is not yet due.

Ties
